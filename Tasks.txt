# Megathon AI Claims Assessment Plan

## Step 1: Image Damage Detection
- Train CNN model on car damage dataset (severity classification)
- Output: {No Damage, Minor, Moderate, Severe}

## Step 2: Cost Band Estimation
- Simple mapping: Minor → Low cost, Moderate → Medium, Severe → High

## Step 3: Fraud Detection
- Compare image features with known tampered dataset (CASIA)
- Detect suspicious patterns

## Step 4: Explainability
- Use Grad-CAM to visualize damaged areas

## Step 5: UI
- Build Streamlit interface:
  - Upload image
  - Show predicted severity + cost
  - Display Grad-CAM region
  - Fraud alert message
